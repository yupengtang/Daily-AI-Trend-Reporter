### 4. Eliciting Weak-to-Strong Generalization with On-Policy Reverse Distillation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.08798)

On-Policy Reverse Distillation enables stronger models to exceed weak supervisors by amplifying verifier-supported policy gradients along the teacher's shift direction, accelerating optimization without imposing capacity limits.

### 7. GE-Act 2.0: Pretraining and Scaling a World-Action Model for Robotic Manipulation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.05588)

GE-Act 2.0 is a world-action model trained from scratch with a control-oriented autoencoder, single-step visual planner, and inverse dynamics model, using knowledge-aligned selective optimization to enable scalable zero-shot robot manipulation across diverse skills and conditions.

### 8. Miles v0.1: Production-Level Post-Training

### 9. Mask Forcing: Improving Autoregressive Video Diffusion Distillation via Dual-Noise Masking Rollout

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.09123)

Mask Forcing mitigates mode collapse in distilled autoregressive video diffusion by injecting masked cleaner signals during self-rollout, improving visual quality without extra training data.

### 10. Marigold V2: Revisiting Diffusion Transformers for Monocular Depth Estimation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.08084)

Marigold V2 repurposes diffusion transformers for monocular depth estimation via single-step flow-matching inference, semantic alignment, and a Sinkhorn-based two-stage fine-tuning protocol, yielding sharper out-of-distribution depth maps and strong results on related dense regression tasks.
