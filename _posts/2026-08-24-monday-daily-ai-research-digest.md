### 1. InfinityEdit: Infinite Video Editing with a Lightweight Edit-Ignition Adapter

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.20910)

InfinityEdit enables continuous, unbounded video editing by using a lightweight adapter with history, temporal causal, and edit cross-attention modules to extend edits into future streaming frames while maintaining stability.

### 2. Graph Engineering in the Era of LLM Agents: From Individual Intelligence to System Intelligence

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.21156)

Graph Engineering organizes multi-agent LLM systems through dynamic graph structures to coordinate specialized agents and manage complex, evolving tasks.

### 3. Let's Scale Step by Step: Compute-Efficient Hyperparameter Transfer for Large-Scale Mixture-of-Experts

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.20061)

A two-step hyperparameter transfer framework predicts optimal learning rates for large Mixture-of-Experts models by scaling across widths and token budgets, enabling efficient pretraining without costly sweeps.

### 4. ParaTempo: Efficient Parallel Reasoning via Temporal Confidence

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.16425)

ParaTempo improves parallel reasoning efficiency by using temporal confidence to dynamically prune, retire, and reallocate reasoning branches without synchronization.

### 5. OmniAssistBench: Assistant-style Interaction Benchmark for Omni-LLMs

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.21360)

OmniAssistBench evaluates real-time interactive video assistants by reverse-engineering multi-turn interaction videos, revealing that current omni-modal models struggle with visual prompts, context retention, and timely responses.

### 6. Every Coin Has Two Sides: On the Dual Nature of Generalization in On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.16647)

On-policy distillation transfers reasoning behaviors rather than specific answers, with generalization strongly tied to teacher-student origin alignment and multi-teacher combinations causing capability trade-offs.

### 7. EviRank: Structured Relevance Evidence for Multimodal Image Re-ranking

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.20886)

EviRank reformulates multimodal image re-ranking as semantic constraint satisfaction by parsing queries into structured evidence packages and verifying candidates via rubric scoring and listwise comparison without training.

### 8. Beyond Correctness: Benchmarking and Aligning Response Behaviors in Hybrid-Thinking MLLMs

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.12781)

Hybrid-thinking multimodal language models suffer from response-pattern misalignment between thinking and non-thinking modes, which is addressed by a diagnostic benchmark and pattern-specific reinforcement learning penalties.

### 9. UniSpace: Unified Visual Representation and Scalable Multimodal Modeling

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.08676)

A reparameterized pretrained vision transformer unifies semantic understanding, high-fidelity reconstruction, and image generation within a single visual space without requiring a separate VAE.

### 10. AgentMercury: Your Agent Can Synthesize Verifiable Environments for Business Scenarios at scale

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.20634)

AgentMercury synthesizes scalable executable business environments that serve as generalizable reinforcement learning substrates, improving agent performance across enterprise and out-of-domain reasoning tasks while making environment construction itself learnable.
