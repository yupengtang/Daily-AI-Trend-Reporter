### 1. StreamEval: Evaluating Interactive Streaming Video Generation Models

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28402)

StreamEval benchmarks interactive streaming video generation across five tasks, revealing that reliable request execution and sustained state consistency remain major challenges despite high visual quality.

### 2. SpatialScout: Can VLM Agents Actively Explore and Map 3D Scenes?

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26659)

SpatialScout evaluates vision-language agents on active 3D object mapping, revealing major gaps in precise metric map construction.

### 3. DONIC: Generalist Dexterous Bimanual Tracking Control from Human Demonstrations

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29997)

DONIC is a generalist bimanual tracker trained on diverse human-motion data that uses reinforcement learning with recycled demonstrations and per-hand rewards to achieve high success on unseen objects without fine-tuning.

### 4. Cache Engineering for Agents

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28577)

AKV introduces cache engineering to manage LLM agent key-value caches without altering trajectories, improving efficiency and accuracy via drop and repos operations with a co-designed serving engine.

### 5. Looking Ahead: Modeling Future Evolution in Autoregressive Video Generation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29989)

Future Chunk Prediction improves autoregressive video diffusion by enforcing future-chunk supervision, yielding more physically plausible video generation with reduced training cost.

### 6. Agent World Model in the Wild: Computer-Use Agent Environment Synthesis

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26828)

WildAWM synthesizes controllable computer-use environments and tasks to train general agents via interaction, improving out-of-distribution performance across web, CLI, and GUI benchmarks.

### 7. Right Screen, Wrong Transition: World Models as Verifiers for GUI Agents

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.11942)

A decoder-free, action-conditioned world model predicts next-screen representations to verify GUI transitions and detect hijacked screens with high accuracy and low latency.

### 8. PrismaCoder: Pushing the Boundaries of Open Multimodal Code Intelligence through Decomposed Rubric Synthesis

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.20538)

PrismaForge uses a rubric-based pipeline to synthesize and filter high-quality multimodal code data, enabling open models that rival proprietary systems on visual coding tasks.

### 9. Scaling in Games: Continued Pre-Training For Embodied Agents In Diverse Virtual Worlds

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28698)

Continued pre-training across data scale, diversity, and model size improves embodied learning in virtual worlds but reveals limits in distant-world generalization, motivating richer training beyond imitation.

### 10. Spectral-Spatial Interpretation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.21114)

Spectral-Spatial Interpretation assigns localized channel importance at each spatial position via conditional Shapley values, yielding full spectral-spatial attribution matrices that outperform existing methods on multispectral image tasks.
