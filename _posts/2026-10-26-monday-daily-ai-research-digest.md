### 1. Reading, Writing, Sharing, and Interacting with the World through Video

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31668)

This survey unifies video understanding and generation through world modeling, organizing progress around inferring, synthesizing, sharing, and interacting with latent temporal states to emphasize persistent dynamics over clip-level perception.

### 2. UserArena: Benchmarking Interactive User Simulation for Long-Horizon Shopping Agents

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.30932)

UserArena introduces a real-world interactive benchmark with long-horizon shopping trajectories and an evaluation framework, alongside On-Policy Trajectory Distillation to improve simulator fidelity for agentic recommendations.

### 3. HiSTEVE 1.0: Harnessing Human-Interface VLA Agents for Open-World Games

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31162)

HiSTEVE is a hierarchical vision-language-action agent that combines long-horizon planning with native keyboard-and-mouse control to perform complex open-ended tasks in Minecraft.

### 4. RSIHub: A Composable Framework for Agent-Evolution Methods

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.32253)

RSIHub is an open-source framework that standardizes agent-evolution research by providing shared infrastructure for building, comparing, and extending methods with reusable components and consistent evaluation.

### 5. RynnUnify: Towards Unified Embodied Brain

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.32965)

RynnUnify is a 16B unified embodied model that integrates visual planning, physical reasoning, and robot control, achieving state-of-the-art results on long-horizon embodied tasks.

### 6. Omni-Woodpecker: Hallucination Correction via Evidence-Prior Rebalancing for Omni LLMs

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.27103)

Omni-Woodpecker is a training-free framework that reduces cross-modal hallucinations in omni LLMs by identifying modality conflicts and rebalancing hidden representations to suppress misleading priors.

### 7. Every Weight Is a Path of Shared Discrete Decisions

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31931)

TWIG represents neural network weights as shared discrete decision paths, unifying compression methods and enabling efficient fixed-weight circuits with optimized discrete choices.

### 8. Dual Exam: Controlled Evaluation of Multimodal Agents and World Models

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31066)

Dual Exam evaluates multimodal agents and world models separately across diverse domains, revealing uneven performance and no coupled success in pilot tests.

### 9. DirCast: Live Esports Directing and Commentary with a Single Streaming Video-Language Model

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.31734)

DirCast is a streaming video language model that simultaneously directs live esports broadcasts by selecting cameras and generates real-time commentary using only past context.

### 10. TenetBench: A Clause-Anchored Russian Corpus for Evaluating Language Models against Professional Codes of Ethics

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29477)

TenetBench evaluates whether language models adhere to professional ethical codes by pairing conflicting requests with benign equivalents, revealing frequent unflagged violations across most tested models.
