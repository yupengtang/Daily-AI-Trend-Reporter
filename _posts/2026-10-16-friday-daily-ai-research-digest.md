### 1. OmniCompass: A Holistic Benchmark for Omni-Modal Video Grounding and Understanding

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18829)

OmniCompass is a holistic benchmark for omni-modal video grounding and understanding that evaluates vision-language and audio-visual reasoning through temporal grounding, spatial grounding, and question answering tasks.

### 2. Waypoint-OPD: Transferring RL-Induced Policy Shifts via Gated On-Policy Distillation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.21601)

Waypoint-OPD improves policy-shift distillation by gating endpoint shifts using directional agreement between early and late teacher policy changes, boosting reasoning performance with minimal overhead.

### 3. OpenGCUA: Toward a Fully Open Stack for Generalist Computer Use

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22200)

OpenGCUA introduces an open generalist computer-use agent that integrates visual and programmatic interfaces, harness-to-model decision transfer, and code execution to improve task performance and efficiency across models.

### 4. FDE-Gym: The Last Mile of AI Delivery

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22350)

FDE-Gym evaluates AI agents on realistic customer delivery tasks requiring clarification of ambiguous requirements and discovery of unstated constraints, revealing a substantial performance gap compared to well-specified benchmarks.

### 5. RoTA-Bench: Evaluating 3D Spatial Reasoning through Interactive Projection Alignment

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17665)

Vision-language models struggle to convert 2D visual observations into precise 3D rotational control, showing large gaps versus humans in projection alignment tasks.

### 6. Self-Evolving Agent Harness

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18813)

A framework for self-evolving agent harnesses uses feedback-driven revisions and evolutionary optimization with theoretical generalization guarantees while keeping model weights fixed.

### 7. MJev-Spatial: System One Spatial Intelligence via Native Attention Matching

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22276)

MJev-Spatial is a fast multimodal decision model that unifies spatial reasoning tasks into a single-pass classification framework, improving speed and accuracy over autoregressive vision-language models.

### 8. Learning Where to Split: Dynamic Band Splitting for Audio Source Separation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.14993)

Dynamic Band Splitting adapts frequency partition boundaries to each audio mixture, improving source separation quality while maintaining a fixed token budget.

### 9. InfiniSplat v2: Analyzing and Improving Feed-Forward 3DGS from Any Views

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.20341)

InfiniSplat v2 improves feed-forward 3D Gaussian reconstruction by decoupling geometry and attribute prediction, aligning Gaussians with ground-truth poses, and using large-angle supervision to enhance novel-view rendering quality.

### 10. 3DCodeVerse: Exploring 3D Worlds as Code

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22341)

3DCodeVerse is a research ecosystem that provides datasets, automated verification harnesses, and empirical guidance to enable scalable, editable, and verifiable 3D content generation via executable code.
