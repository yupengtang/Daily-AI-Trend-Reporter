### 1. Scaling Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning for Brain Encoding

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26802)

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning with low-rank adapters enables efficient adaptation of very large multimodal backbones for brain encoding, improving performance and interpretability while reducing computational cost.

### 2. MemT-1.0: Towards Memory-Native Evolution

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26825)

MemT-1.0 introduces a memory-native architecture that embeds continuous memory into foundation model computation, improving long-context efficiency and agentic task performance.

### 3. Learning from Fleet Experience: A Collective Learning Paradigm for Robots

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25736)

Robots share execution experience across fleets to improve policies via failure-guided retrieval and asynchronous training, achieving high success without extra expert corrections.

### 4. Holi-World: Building Holistic World Intelligence from Human-Ego Experience

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26549)

HOLI-WORLD is an integrated ecosystem that automates the construction of large-scale learning resources from egocentric recordings to improve machine understanding of dynamic scenes and interactions.

### 5. EgoXperience: Learning World-Grounded Egocentric Representations from Human Experience

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26625)

EgoXperience introduces a unified framework for joint reconstruction of camera motion, scene geometry, hands, and objects from egocentric video using automatically annotated large-scale data and a single-pass model.

### 6. OR-License: Evaluating LLM Agents as Real-World Operations Research Practitioners

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.24087)

OR-License evaluates whether LLM agents can solve real-world operations research problems from original client materials, finding that while some achieve high objective quality, none match expert-level feasibility and deployment-adjusted quality.

### 7. ScaleNav: Scalable Internet Video Pretraining for Language-Conditioned Local Navigation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25690)

ScaleNav learns goal-directed visual dynamics from internet egocentric videos to generate robot actions for language-conditioned local navigation, achieving high success rates in simulation and real-world deployment.

### 8. SpecMRL: Redesign Matryoshka Representation Learning within Parametric Spectrum

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26824)

SpecMRL improves compressed embeddings by reorganizing features via a learned spectral basis that prioritizes informative directions, outperforming standard MRL at reduced dimensions.

### 9. OpenVLHarness: Real World Visual Agentic Tool-Use with Multimodal Memory

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26860)

OpenVLHarness improves multimodal reasoning by integrating persistent memory, heterogeneous tool interfaces, and iterative evidence composition across 22 benchmarks.

### 10. Self-Evolving Coding Agents via Harness-Policy Optimization

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22791)

Self-HPO is a recursive framework that alternates harness and policy optimization for coding agents, improving performance by targeting evolving failure modes through reinforcement learning.
