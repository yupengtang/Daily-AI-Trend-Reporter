### 1. FrameForge: Harnessing Priors of Video Foundation Models for Unified Image Generation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36545)

FrameForge enables adaptive conditional image generation by modeling variable condition-target relationships through dynamic temporal experts and intermediate reasoning pathways in pretrained video models.

### 2. R^2Orch: Recursive Self-Improvement for Agent Orchestration via Self-Play within a Skill-Internalization Loop

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22740)

R²Orch uses recursive self-play with learnable task selection and skill withdrawal to train orchestrators without hand-designed curricula, improving performance across reasoning and interactive tasks.

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.39497)

### 4. REA: A Routed Embodied Agent Runtime for Asynchronous Brain-Cerebellum Control

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.42053)

### 6. WeFair: Fidelity-Aware Alignment from Human Feedback for Personalized Query Rewriting in Short-Video Search
