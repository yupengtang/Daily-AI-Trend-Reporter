### 1. Route Anything: A Study of Model and Policy Routing for General-Purpose AI Systems

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05275)

Route Anything is a framework for dynamically selecting and coordinating specialized AI models and policies across diverse tasks to improve performance and efficiency.

### 2. Words Caught in the Heart: Simulating Person-Specific Expression for Self-Opaque Clients in Psychotherapy

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.00617)

Pandora is a psychotherapy client simulation framework that models evolving psychological and relational states to generate more authentic, individually aligned expressions and reduce AI-like responses.

### 3. Think Less, Locally: Local Preference Learning for Reasoning Compression

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04814)

LoCo uses local preference learning to teach reasoning models when to stop deliberating, reducing output length substantially while preserving or improving accuracy across math and code tasks.

### 4. MoviEdit: Long-form Multi-Shot Video Editing Made Easy

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.02737)

MoviEdit is an end-to-end framework for long-form multi-shot video editing that uses cascaded bidirectional-autoregressive modeling and a new benchmark to maintain coherence across scenes while reducing manual data annotation.

### 5. Behind Video Generation: Observations and Insights on Multimodal Understanding from Kling

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04916)

Kling's multimodal understanding stack links caption density, perceptual text encoding, reward modeling, and evaluation protocols to improve controllable video generation.

### 6. DreamX: Video Diffusion Model is a Universal Latent Action Learner

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05377)

DreamX leverages a pretrained video diffusion model to learn compact, appearance-invariant latent actions that enable cross-embodiment transfer and efficient policy learning.

### 7. RoboColosseum: Evaluating Physical Intelligence at Scale In-The-Wild

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.02497)

RoboColosseum evaluates general robot policies across diverse embodiments and environments, revealing weak cross-embodiment agreement, limited open-world reliability, and substantial differences between zero-shot and fine-tuned performance.

### 8. Factorized MLLM Embeddings for Composed Image Retrieval

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05193)

FactEmbed improves composed image retrieval by decoupling query understanding from embedding construction through factorized latents that simulate visual modifications before retrieval.

### 9. Axion 1.0: Open Agent Foundation Models, Simplified

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03233)

Axion 1.0 is an open agent foundation model trained via a concise three-stage pipeline of supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, achieving competitive agentic performance while fully releasing its data and training recipe.

### 10. ToolSpace: Scaling Tool-Centric Environment Synthesis for Long-Horizon Agents

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05036)

ToolSpace constructs realistic multi-environment tool-use benchmarks by mining execution traces, clustering tool compositions, and validating tasks through replayable state-aware random walks.
