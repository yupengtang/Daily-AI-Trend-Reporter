[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.10915)

Combodied Agents integrate digital and embodied tools into a closed-loop framework that models individual human-state trajectories over time to provide proportionate, consent-aware support.

### 2. Co-Evolution in Agentic Systems: Toward Self-Directed Evolution Beyond Human Design

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.10299)

Agentic systems can achieve open-ended improvement through multi-component co-evolution that progressively removes fixed human constraints across agents, environments, and evolution mechanisms.

### 3. Beyond Pixels: From Video Priors to 4D Worlds

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.10744)

Latent-to-4D enables reusable direct 4D generation from video diffusion latents via alignment with a pretrained decoder and spatiotemporal attention, transferring across generators without retraining.

### 4. Articulated Object Reconstruction from Rest-State Observation

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.27749)

A rest-state framework reconstructs articulated objects from a single closed configuration by fusing vision-language outputs into consistent part meshes and validating synthesized motion hypotheses via geometric consistency.

### 5. AdvFD: Boosting Visual Generation via Adversarial Fr'echet Distance Loss

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.11205)

Adversarial Fréchet Distance improves generator post-training by adding a learnable adversarial feature space to static Fréchet losses, with whitening to stabilize optimization.

### 6. Mendel Gödel Machine: Recursive Self-Improving Coding Agents via Comparative Evolution

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.07645)

Mendel Gödel Machine improves self-improving coding agents by using multi-trajectory mutations and cross-lineage hybridization to accelerate convergence and boost performance.

### 7. VibeLifeBench: Can Your Life Agent Be Proactive and Persistent in a Living World?

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.10875)

A new benchmark called VibeLifeBench evaluates long-horizon proactive agents across simulated multi-week everyday tasks, revealing that current frontier models perform poorly.

### 8. Ex-Omni-2D: Expressive Omni-Modal Dialogue Models with Native Visual Presence

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.10720)

Ex-Omni-2D is an omni-modal dialogue framework that produces coordinated text, speech, and video responses via a visual thought plan and a distilled streaming video generator.

### 9. Decoding-Level Taboo: A Diagnostic Stress Test for LLM Robustness

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.09900)

Decoding-Level Taboo is a runtime logit-space stress test that reveals how large language models handle off-nominal generation paths, showing that robustness depends on scale and instruction alignment.

### 10. SkillZip: Evaluation-Free Skill Compression for Self-Evolving Agents by Discovering Reusable Structure

[Read Paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.11079)

SkillZip compresses self-evolving agent skills by finding a minimal faithful structural explanation that shares repeated rules and procedures while preserving rare exceptions, without requiring evaluation rollouts.
